Opening a practice means Medicare enrollment, CAQH, commercial plans and a calendar of renewals, all before the first claim is paid. We prepare each application as a complete, ready-to-file packet. You review it and click submit from your own accounts. We never ask for, store or use your portal passwords.

How it works

  1. Free checklist and quote. Tell us your state, whether you'll bill Medicare, and the commercial plans you want to bill; we send the enrollment checklist for your practice, typical timelines, and a written quote.
  2. You share your documents in a folder you own: Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox.
  3. We prepare the packet. Application by application, with every field filled, every attachment listed, and plain step-by-step instructions for submitting.
  4. You submit it. You or your authorized official clicks submit in PECOS, CAQH and each commercial plan's portal. Your credentials stay yours.
  5. We keep it current. We track CAQH re-attestation, license, malpractice and Medicare revalidation dates, and send you each update ready to approve.
