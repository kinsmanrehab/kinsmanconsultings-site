Letter Rack privacy policy

Effective [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]

This policy covers Letter Rack, the desktop app for Mac and Windows, and the Letter Rack pages at kinsmanconsultings.com/letterrack/, including asking for early access. Letter Rack is provided by Kinsman Consulting LLC, a Massachusetts company ("we" and "us").

The short version. The app sends us nothing. Your records stay on your computer, and we can't see them. The only information about you that reaches us is what you choose to email us. Our website host also keeps logs of visits and downloads, which we don't receive.

What the app doesn't collect

Letter Rack has no account and no sign-in. It has no analytics, tracking or advertising, and no AI connector. It has no updater, and it doesn't connect to us. We never receive your statements, budget, reports, passphrase or recovery code, and we never hold a copy of your records.

Where your records live

Letter Rack keeps your records in a folder on your computer, encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Only your passphrase, your recovery code, or the Touch ID or Windows Hello unlock you set up on that computer can open them. Touch ID and Windows Hello are checked by macOS or Windows. Letter Rack never sees your fingerprint or face. If you turn either on, its key is kept in that computer's own key store: the Mac keychain, set to that Mac only, or Windows Credential Manager. Backups are encrypted files that you save where you choose.

We can't see, recover or delete your records for you. There's no passphrase reset. They're yours to back up, move or delete.

Some things Letter Rack keeps on your computer aren't encrypted:

  • the reports and exports you save, and the statements you downloaded from your bank (Letter Rack keeps its own encrypted copy and leaves your originals where they were);
  • your display settings: theme, text size and Quiet names;
  • a short crash note, if Letter Rack stops unexpectedly. It holds the version, your type of computer and where in Letter Rack's code it stopped, with long numbers removed. Letter Rack shows it to you the next time you open it, and you can delete it or email it to us;
  • on Windows, a crash file, if Letter Rack's window crashes. Windows' web component writes it to a folder on your computer, and it can hold part of what was on screen. Letter Rack doesn't send it anywhere.

Your computer's own services

Letter Rack runs on parts of macOS and Windows that Apple and Microsoft provide. Those parts follow Apple's and Microsoft's privacy terms and your system settings. We don't receive anything they collect.

  • When you download and first open Letter Rack, your computer's security checks may contact Apple or Microsoft about the app.
  • On Windows, Letter Rack's window runs in Microsoft's WebView2, Windows' own web component. That component's crash reports stay on your computer. Like the rest of Windows, it sends Microsoft the diagnostic data your Windows privacy settings allow. Microsoft collects some of that whatever the setting; you can limit the rest under Settings › Privacy & security › Diagnostics & feedback. On some older Windows 10 computers, the installer downloads WebView2 from Microsoft.
  • If an app stops unexpectedly, your computer may send a crash report of its own: macOS to Apple, if you've chosen to share analytics with Apple, and Windows to Microsoft, as your diagnostic settings allow. These reports come from macOS or Windows, not from Letter Rack.

If you email us

You might email us to ask for early access, to send feedback, a crash note or a report on a statement layout Letter Rack couldn't read, or to ask a question. The app's feedback and report buttons open your own email app. You see every word, and nothing goes unless you send it. We receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, and what you wrote. Please don't send us statements, account numbers or your passphrase.

We use what you send only to reply, to fix and improve Letter Rack, and for what you wrote to us about. We keep it only as long as we need it for that, and we delete it sooner if you ask.

Early access

Your email comes to desk@kinsmanconsultings.com and is kept in our email account (Gmail). We use your address for one thing: a single email when Letter Rack, or the phone view, is ready. We don't add it to any other list, newsletter or survey, and we don't share it. We delete these emails within 30 days of sending the "ready" email. Ask us at any time and we'll delete yours within two working days and confirm by reply.

This website

The Letter Rack pages have no analytics, cookies, tracking or ads. They load no fonts, scripts or images from anywhere else.

The pages and the Letter Rack downloads are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub delivers them through Fastly, a content delivery company that works for GitHub. When you visit a page or download Letter Rack, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive those logs.

Do Not Track. These pages don't track you, so every visit is treated the same, with or without a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. No other company tracks you across sites through them.

Who else sees your information

We don't sell, rent or share your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. These companies handle it for us, each under its own privacy terms:

GitHub (website and download hosting, delivered through Fastly), IONOS (forwarding email to our account), Google (our Gmail account), and Anthropic, whose AI assistant, Claude, helps us sort and answer our email.

We'd disclose information to anyone else only if the law requires it, such as a valid court order.

Children

Letter Rack and these pages are meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has emailed us, tell us and we'll delete it.

Security

The app's protection is described above. On our side, emails are kept in one account, protected by a strong password and two-step sign-in. Letter Rack's code is not open source and hasn't been independently audited yet. No email or website is perfectly secure. If we learn that someone got into information we hold about you, we'll tell you promptly.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you (usually just your email address and what you wrote). You can also ask us to correct it or delete it. Email desk@kinsmanconsultings.com. We'll answer within 30 days and delete within two working days. Asking won't change how we treat you. We can't reach your Letter Rack records. They're only on your computer.

We're a Massachusetts company. Massachusetts doesn't yet have a general consumer privacy law; if one passes that applies to us, we'll update this policy. Based on our size, we don't believe California's Consumer Privacy Act, or similar laws in other states, apply to us. They cover larger businesses, or businesses that sell personal information, which we don't. We give everyone the rights above anyway.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. A new version takes effect on the date it shows and applies only from then on. A change will never let us use your early-access email address for anything but the one "ready" email. We'll post each new version on this page with its date. Ask us for an earlier version and we'll send it.
